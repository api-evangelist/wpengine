As editorial operations mature, it’s common for publishing workflows to become fragmented across teams, processes, and systems, creating points of friction that can be difficult to unpick. The effects are felt throughout the entire publishing journey. Fragmented workflows slow editorial teams, impede collaboration, and create inconsistencies in how content is reviewed, approved, and published.
