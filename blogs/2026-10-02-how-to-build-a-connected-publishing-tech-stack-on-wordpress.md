For enterprise teams publishing on WordPress®, the open source CMS provides the ideal foundation for creating, managing, and publishing content. But it’s rarely the only system involved. Behind the scenes, planning might happen in one platform, drafting in another, with separate systems for managing digital assets, coordinating approvals, checking analytics, and handling distribution.
