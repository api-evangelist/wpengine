AI-driven search experiences are completely reshaping how websites are discovered, interpreted, and cited. Discovery is happening in AI summaries within traditional search engines, conversational AI tools, and generative answer engines. As user habits shift toward these direct-answer formats, a brand’s visibility now depends on how clearly machines can access, parse, and interpret its digital presence.
